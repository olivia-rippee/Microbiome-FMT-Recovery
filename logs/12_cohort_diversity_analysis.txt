============================================================
PHASE 12 CONCLUSION
============================================================

Cohort-wide alpha and beta diversity metrics were calculated
after rarefaction to 7,500 reads per sample.

This sampling depth retained all 208 samples and all 52
participants with complete four-timepoint longitudinal data.

Alpha diversity included observed features, Shannon
diversity, Faith's phylogenetic diversity, and evenness.

Beta diversity included Bray-Curtis, Jaccard, weighted
UniFrac, and unweighted UniFrac distances.

Within-participant distances from each recipient's own
pre-FMT baseline were calculated for 7d, 14d, and 30d.

These outputs are ready for repeated-measures cohort-level
statistical analysis.
