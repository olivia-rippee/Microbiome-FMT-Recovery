PHASE 14 CONCLUSION
============================================================

Cohort-wide differential abundance was evaluated using
ANCOM-BC at genus and family taxonomic levels.

The model included both timepoint and participant:

    abundance ~ timepoint + participant

Pre-FMT samples were used as the reference condition.

Participant was included as a blocking covariate to account
for persistent inter-individual differences in microbial
composition.

Taxa present in fewer than 10% of samples were excluded
using the ANCOM-BC prevalence filter.

Benjamini-Hochberg adjustment was used to control the
false-discovery rate.

Genus-level results represent the primary differential
abundance analysis; family-level results provide a
secondary taxonomic summary.
