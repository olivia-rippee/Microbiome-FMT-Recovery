PHASE 13 CONCLUSION
============================================================

Repeated-measures longitudinal statistical analyses were
performed across the complete 52-participant FMT cohort.

Alpha-diversity changes were evaluated using paired
non-parametric tests and participant-level mixed-effects
models.

Benjamini-Hochberg correction was applied across the six
pairwise timepoint comparisons within each alpha metric.

Beta-diversity displacement was calculated relative to
each participant's own pre-FMT baseline.

Post-FMT baseline distances were compared across 7-, 14-,
and 30-day timepoints using paired longitudinal tests.

These analyses account for the repeated-measures structure
of the longitudinal cohort.
